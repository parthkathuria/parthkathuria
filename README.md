## About me

- 🛠️ **Senior Software Engineer** with **10+ years** building platform modernization programs, developer tooling, and large-scale distributed systems.
- 🤖 I work across **AI-native developer tooling** — agentic migration pipelines, language servers (LSP), and VS Code extensions — and **distributed systems at scale**.
- 🏢 Previously at **Intuit** (TurboTax/ProSeries platform & tooling) and **NEXTracker** (IoT telemetry across **650k+** devices).
- ⚡ I make complex migrations safe and fast — pairing deterministic engineering with AI where it earns its place, and measuring quality instead of trusting it.
- 🔭 **Open to senior & staff engineering roles** in distributed systems, AI infrastructure, developer tooling, and platforms.
- 🌐 More at **[parthkathuria.pages.dev](https://parthkathuria.pages.dev)**
